KINGSLASH Privacy Policy

Effective: [2026-11-18] · Last updated: 2026-11-17

KINGSLASH (the "Game") does not collect any personal information. This policy explains what the Game stores on your device and how.

1. Personal information collected

The Game does not collect or transmit any personal information such as your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. There is no sign-up or login.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (shards, upgrades, unlocks, achievements, records, save points) and settings (sound, music, vibration, quality, language) are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to anyone, including the developer, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The save code in "Save Data" is text you copy and keep yourself; the Game does not send it anywhere.

3. Third parties and external services

The Game contains no advertising, analytics, payments or server communication, so no information is shared with third parties.

4. Permissions

The Game requests no device permissions other than vibration.

5. Children's privacy

The Game does not collect personal information from users of any age.

6. Changes

If advertising, analytics or online features are added, this policy will be revised and the effective date updated.
