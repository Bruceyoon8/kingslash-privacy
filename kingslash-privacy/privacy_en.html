KINGSLASH Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-23 · Last updated: 2026-11-23

The developer of KINGSLASH (the "Game") does not directly collect or keep personal information such as your name, email or phone number. The Game does include ads (Google AdMob), in-app purchases (Google Play Billing) and an optional Google account sign-in for cloud save and rankings (Google Firebase), which process information as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (shards, feathers, upgrades, unlocks, achievements, records, check-in and daily missions, save points), your settings (sound, music, vibration, quality) and which purchased items you own are stored only on your device. Unless you sign in (section 2), they are not sent anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The save code in "Save management" is text you copy and keep yourself; the Game does not send it anywhere.

2. Google sign-in, cloud save and rankings (optional)

Signing in is optional; every feature of the Game works without it. If you sign in with Google under "Account · Rank", the following is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database):

  • Account identifiers: a Firebase user ID, plus the email address and name of the Google account you signed in with (kept by Firebase Authentication; never shown in the Game or in rankings)
  • Cloud save: your game progress and settings from section 1 (to restore them on a new device or after reinstalling)
  • Rankings: the nickname you choose and your best records (Expedition, King's Tower, Ash Rush). Nicknames and records are visible to other players.

This information is used only for cloud save and rankings, never for ads or other purposes, and is kept until you delete your account.

Account deletion: tap "Account · Rank" → "Delete account" in the Game to permanently delete your sign-in account, cloud save and ranking records immediately. If you have already uninstalled the app, email us at the address below with the Google account you signed in with; we will delete the data within 30 days. Signing out alone keeps your progress on the device.

2-1. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded and interstitial ads. To serve ads and measure their performance, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, OS, language) and approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and clicks

Google processes this information under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires consent (for example in Europe), the Game shows Google's consent form before showing ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

If you buy "Remove Ads", interstitial ads stop and rewarded bonuses are granted without watching an ad.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Paid items are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and payment details; the developer never receives your card number or other payment details. The Game only uses the purchase information Google Play provides (item, purchase state, purchase identifier) to confirm purchases and deliver items.

4. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or share your information with third parties. Apart from the Google services in sections 2 (Firebase), 2-1 (AdMob) and 3 (Play Billing), the Game does not send information to analytics tools or external servers.

5. Permissions

The Game uses vibration, internet access (ads, purchases, sign-in, cloud save and rankings) and the advertising ID permission. It does not request other device permissions.

6. Children's privacy

This app is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and we delete such information promptly if we become aware of it.

7. Changes

If how information is handled changes, this policy will be updated along with its last-updated date.
